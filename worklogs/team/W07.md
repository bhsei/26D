## Findings
- Several people wrote functional requirements at the same time, so the numbers could collide.
- The prototype showed that a cash sale needs exactly 3 clicks, so NFR-06 was changed to "3 clicks or fewer".
- The reviews of SRS v0.9 found, among other things:
  - the retry rule for the Payment Service was different in IR-02, FR-11, UC1 and UC3;
  - the deposit rule was contradictory for damaged items;
  - some cases were missing (empty sale, invalid quantity, new customer);
  - some requirements had no verification method.
- Three lessons for W08: the integration was late, two merge commits reached main, and some texts were written in two places.

## Decisions
- Functional requirement ID ranges: UC1 FR-01 to FR-19, UC2 FR-20 to FR-29, UC3 FR-30 to FR-39, UC4 FR-40 to FR-49 and UC5 FR-50 to FR-59.
- The deposit of a rental is not taxed (working assumption, to be checked against the full case study).
- The retry of the card payment stays in IR-02, because timeouts are usually temporary and the payment still needs authorization (BR-05). It was added to UC1 ext. 9b2b, FR-11, UC3 ext. 13b2b, FR-37 and AC-05.
- The external systems have timeouts: 10 seconds for the Payment Service and 5 seconds for the Tax Calculator and the HR System (IR-02, IR-05, IR-09).
- PINs are stored only as a salted hash (DR-11), and NFR-08 now talks about PINs.
- Changes for W08: the integration deadline is one day before the reviews, `git pull --rebase` is checked before every push, and texts are copied from the SRS, which is the one source of truth.
- Documents go directly to `main`, one document per commit, with the message format `W7 - summary; description`.
