## Findings
- The reviews found differences between documents, for example: a barcode scanner that works as a keyboard types into the browser and not into the POS application; the close session did not name the rental return as an open transaction; and an open point about the card deposit that was already decided.
- A decided question changes several documents at the same time (the SRS, the glossary, the system interfaces, the sequence diagrams and the architecture), so a consistency pass was needed at the end of the week.
- Still open: how customers are identified (Q5), two registers renting the same item (Q6) and the retention period, currency and rounding (Q8).

## Decisions
- AD-01 layered architecture on each register, AD-02 local web application and AD-03 Java (without a Python fallback) are accepted.
- AD-04 (one adapter for each external system), AD-07 (timeouts and fallbacks), AD-09 and AD-10 (device access: the scanner works as a keyboard, and the card reader, printer and cash drawer are used through a Device Service) are written as decision records. AD-05 and AD-06 follow in W10.
- Values decided for the open questions:
  - Refund period 30 days; rental periods 1, 3 or 7 days; late fee of one day's rental fee for each day late; the deposit is not taxed.
  - The deposit of a card rental is charged and refunded; no offline card authorization.
  - One tender for each sale or rental; no discounts or promotions.
  - A cash difference of up to 5.00 is recorded; a larger one is reviewed by the store manager.
- The SRS prevails over the companion files; the decided values stay marked as Proposed until the full case study is checked.
