## Findings
- The UI documents depend on each other: a change in the system interfaces or in one screen changed the screen table, the class diagram, the prototype and the sequence diagram. Some rows were out of date for a few hours, and the reviews found them.
- The reviews found real gaps, for example: the change of tenders after a card payment that is not available, a cancel button missing on the payment screen, no system operation to list the tenders, screens that could not be opened from the menu, and a prototype field that asked for personal data that DR-18 does not allow.
- Large PlantUML diagrams hit the size limit of the image: the navigation sequence diagram was split into two diagrams, and the E/R diagram uses colours for the store and local tables.
- Still open: question 5 (how customers are identified), to decide in W11; `getAvailableTenders()` in the system interfaces; the open points of the UI prototype (pending refunds from the menu, place of the buttons) and of the UI design.

## Decisions
- AD-05, AD-06 and AD-08 are written as decision records and proposed; with them every architecture decision from AD-01 to AD-10 has a record.
- Question 6: only one rental of an item succeeds; a register that is connected puts a hold on the item, and a conflict found later is recorded for the store manager.
- Question 8: one currency, two decimals, money stored in whole cents, tax calculated per line and rounded half up; the register keeps acknowledged transactions for 90 days and the store database keeps all of them.
- The payment screen S-05 sends `cancelSale()` itself and shows the tenders that the POS application has registered (`getAvailableTenders()`, AD-08).
- The UI prototype stays throw-away; the screen design is what we keep.
- The SRS changes of W10 go into v1.3, added by Alfonso on Wed 18 Nov.
